You read AI roleplay character cards for a library that shows each card by
what a person may come to it for. Under <card_to_read>...</card_to_read> you are
given the card as JSON: its title, its tags and its characters, each with name,
description, personality, scenario, first_mes, alternate_greetings (may be
empty) and mes_example.

Below is a list of needs. Each finishes the sentence "I need…", said by the
person who will chat with this card, not by its character. Read adult content
matter-of-factly, as you read any other.

{needs}

First write, in two sentences, who comes to this card and for what.

Then name the needs of the list this card clearly offers, the strongest first:
as few as the card warrants, at most 5. Stop at the first need the card does not
clearly offer; do not fill the list up to 5. Give each two things, in this order:

1. "quote": copied from the card word for word, the shortest passage, at most
   about 25 words, that best shows the card offers this need. Do not reword or
   tidy it.
2. "level": how much the card offers this need to the person chatting; not how
   sure you are:
   2 clearly offered, in a trait or a scene
   3 what the card is built for: the one need, or at most two, the card exists
     to give; never more than two needs at 3

Most cards offer few of these needs: a need the person could only bring to the
chat themselves, as they could to any card, is left out. A card that offers none
of them has an empty list.

OUTPUT — one JSON object and nothing else, no code fence, no commentary. Each
need by its name before the colon:
{
  "verdict": "classified",
  "reject_reason": "",
  "who": "...",
  "needs": [{ "need": "...", "quote": "...", "level": 2 }]
}

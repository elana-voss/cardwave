# 0.1.3

Small fixes.

- Starting Cardwave while it is open brings its window to the front, and when it cannot start because other programs use its ports, it says so.
- Easier to read: the words on blue buttons and the empty hints in the dark theme, your initials in the light theme.
- Chats: dice, random picks and variables in a sent message stay as they were in every later reply.
- Dates and times in the library and on a character's page follow your language.
- Dictation hears even a single short word, and long sentences without punctuation are read aloud in full.

# 0.1.2

More fixes, from a third round of testing.

- Memories: what you changed or deleted stays when an earlier message is edited, and renaming a character or persona keeps what they remember.
- Chats: words typed while a message is sent stay in the field, dictation keeps its last words when you switch tabs, and dates in greetings follow your language.
- Clearer messages when a model on this computer fails its test, when a download's server fails, and when the update check cannot finish.
- Tabs fit a narrow window, however it got narrow.

# 0.1.1

Fixes from a second round of testing.

- Backups: a restore waits for work still running, and a backup file is named by your own day.
- First run: a provider added in Settings goes on to choosing its models, a refused key is told as refused, and setting up again with a corrected key keeps one provider.
- Chats: a character too long for the model is told, not left out; attached pictures are made smaller once, and stay attached when you leave the chat.
- The password screen and other devices' sign-in in your language, and settings that fit a phone's width.
- Many smaller fixes to texts, tabs, pictures and imports.

# 0.1.0

Cardwave, made anew from the ground up: chat with the characters of your library, with models on this computer or at a provider.

- Your library: import SillyTavern and CCV4 cards or make your own, and find them by their words or their meaning.
- Chats with one character or several, who take turns or reply by themselves, with versions, branches, personas and lorebooks.
- Any provider: paste an API key and Cardwave knows whose it is, or choose a GGUF model on this computer.
- Memory: characters remember what happened in your chats.
- Voices: replies read aloud on this computer in each character's own voice, and dictation.
- Tools, as you switch them on: web search, the files of one folder, pictures.
- Twelve languages, a light and a dark theme, a password and backups.
